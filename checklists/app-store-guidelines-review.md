# App Store Review Guidelines Compliance Checklist

Use this checklist to perform an exhaustive pre-submission review against all five sections of Apple's official App Store Review Guidelines.

---

## 1. Safety (Section 1)
- [ ] **1.1 Objectionable Content**: App contains no defamatory, discriminatory, or harmful content.
- [ ] **1.2 User-Generated Content (UGC)**: If UGC is present:
  - [ ] Content reporting mechanism is available on every post/comment.
  - [ ] User blocking functionality is available.
  - [ ] Terms of Service clearly specify zero tolerance for abusive content.
  - [ ] Moderation response plan in place.
- [ ] **1.3 Kids Category**: If aimed at children under 13, no third-party analytics or behavioral advertising is included.

---

## 2. Performance (Section 2)
- [ ] **2.1 App Completeness**:
  - [ ] App is free of crashes, runtime exceptions, and memory leaks.
  - [ ] Zero placeholder text ("Lorem Ipsum", "Demo String", "Test Product").
  - [ ] All buttons, tabs, and links lead to functional screens.
  - [ ] Working demo login credentials provided in App Store Connect review notes.
- [ ] **2.3 Accurate Metadata**:
  - [ ] Screenshots accurately represent app running on supported devices.
  - [ ] App description and keywords accurately reflect current feature set.

---

## 3. Business (Section 3)
- [ ] **3.1.1 In-App Purchase**: Digital features, memberships, and content unlocked in-app use Apple StoreKit 2.
- [ ] **3.1.2 Auto-Renewing Subscriptions**:
  - [ ] Paywall clearly displays price, duration, renewal terms, and cancellation instructions.
  - [ ] "Restore Purchases" button is prominent and functional.
  - [ ] Functional links to Privacy Policy and Terms of Use (EULA) are displayed on the paywall.

---

## 4. Design (Section 4)
- [ ] **4.0 Design Hierarchy**: App adheres to Apple Human Interface Guidelines for typography, navigation, and touch ergonomics.
- [ ] **4.2 Minimum Functionality**: App is more than a wrapped responsive website; it incorporates native iOS features, offline handling, and rich touch controls.
- [ ] **4.8 Sign in with Apple**: If third-party social logins (Google, Facebook, X) are present, **Sign in with Apple** is offered with equal prominence.

---

## 5. Legal (Section 5)
- [ ] **5.1.1 Data Collection**: A working Privacy Policy URL is provided in App Store Connect and inside app settings.
- [ ] **5.1.1(v) Account Deletion**: Users who create an account can delete their account directly within the app.
- [ ] **5.1.2 Data Use & Sharing**: Data is not shared with third parties without explicit user consent.
